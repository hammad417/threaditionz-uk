# Threaditionz.co.uk — Growth, Visibility, Marketing & 90-Day Launch Plan

**Prepared by:** Ecommerce growth strategy / competitor research / product marketing
**Subject brand:** https://threaditionz.co.uk/ — hand-finished 100% silk pocket squares, cravats & scarves
**Method:** Direct browser inspection of Threaditionz (homepage, 35 collections, 181 products, 10 journal articles, all info pages, pricing, footer/social, checkout signals) + web research on UK and international competitors.
**Date:** 26 June 2026
**Note:** This is a visibility/marketing/launch plan, not an SEO report — search is included only where it supports the wider plan.

## A. Executive Summary

### Current position
Threaditionz is a **well-built but near-silent** premium silk-accessories brand. The storefront is genuinely strong on fundamentals most small brands get wrong — 181 products, 35 collections spanning type/colour/pattern/occasion, 10 on-topic journal guides, rich schema, deliberate metadata, free UK delivery over £50, wishlist and accounts. Pricing (≈£12–£82) sits in an attractive "accessible-premium" gap **below** Rampley & Co (£85–£155) and Cravat Club (≈£45–£95) but **above** volume players like DQT (£5–£15). The brand has a genuine, ownable differentiator that it currently underplays: **South Asian heritage artistry** — Ajrak block-print motifs, Islamic calligraphy, and Urdu poetry prints (e.g., *Diwan-e-Ghalib*, *Huroof*, *Gulmohar*) — finished to a British tailoring standard.

The problem is not the product or the website. **The problem is that almost nobody is being driven to it.** The brand behaves as if "build it and they will come."

### Biggest visibility problems (verified)
1. **No working social presence linked from the site.** The `.co.uk` footer's Instagram/Facebook/Pinterest icons point to bare `instagram.com` / `facebook.com` / `pinterest.com` homepages — not brand profiles. An actual account (`@threaditionz`) exists on Instagram but is **disconnected from the UK store**, so social proof and traffic are leaking.
2. **No email capture.** No newsletter pop-up, no welcome offer, no lead magnet detected. Every non-converting visitor is lost forever — fatal for a considered, gifting-led category.
3. **Dual-domain brand split.** A second storefront exists at `threaditionz.com` (Shopify, `/products/...` URLs) alongside `threaditionz.co.uk` (custom, `/product/...`). This splits brand authority, ad-pixel data, reviews/social proof, and confuses attribution.
4. **No marketplace footprint.** Competitors appear on Amazon (DQT), Etsy, eBay, and Not On The High Street; Threaditionz appears on none of the high-intent gifting/wedding marketplaces.
5. **The heritage story is buried.** The most distinctive, PR-worthy, content-rich asset (Ajrak/calligraphy/Ghalib poetry, made by South Asian artisans, finished in England) is not the spearhead of the brand's marketing.
6. **No social proof on-site** (reviews excluded from this plan by request, but UGC, press, and "as worn at" proof are absent).

### Biggest market opportunities
- **Weddings & groomsmen** — the single largest, highest-intent occasion in this category. The brand already has matching cravat/square/scarf sets (the exact thing wedding parties buy) and four wedding journal articles. This is a ready-made wedge.
- **Heritage / cultural gifting** — Eid, South Asian weddings, Ramadan, Father's Day, and the "meaningful luxury gift" angle. Almost no premium competitor owns the calligraphy/Ajrak/poetry niche.
- **Pinterest + short-form video** — "how to fold a pocket square," "how to tie a cravat," and "complete the wedding look" are evergreen, highly saved/shared formats the brand's existing journal videos can feed.
- **Accessible-premium price gap** — undercutting Rampley/Drake's while out-classing DQT/Amazon on story and finish.

### Highest-impact recommendations (do these first)
| # | Move | Why it's top priority |
|---|------|----------------------|
| 1 | Connect & activate Instagram + Pinterest + TikTok (fix footer links to real `@threaditionz`) | Stops traffic/proof leakage; turns existing content into a channel |
| 2 | Add email capture + welcome offer + flows (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase) | Converts the traffic you already have; compounding asset |
| 3 | Resolve the `.com` vs `.co.uk` split (canonical or clear market separation) | Consolidates authority, ad data, and budget |
| 4 | Lead with the heritage story (Ajrak / calligraphy / Ghalib) across brand, PR, content | The only thing competitors can't copy |
| 5 | Launch a Weddings & Groomsmen campaign + bundle (matching sets) | Highest-intent revenue with assets already in place |
| 6 | List best-sellers on Etsy, NOTHS, and Amazon Handmade | Borrowed high-intent traffic while owned channels build |

## B. Threaditionz Website & Brand Analysis

### Brand positioning
"Hand-finished 100% silk pocket squares, cravats and scarves, crafted in England for the modern gentleman. Heritage craftsmanship, made to be remembered." The on-site positioning is **understated British heritage menswear**. The deeper, more distinctive truth — South Asian print heritage (Ajrak, calligraphy, Urdu poetry) executed to a British finish — is present in the *product names* but absent from the *positioning headline*. This is the brand's biggest untapped equity.

### Product range (verified)
- **Types:** Pocket squares (incl. Solid and Four-in-One), Cravats, Silk Scarves, Scarf+Square and Cravat+Square Sets, Luxe Collection.
- **Scale:** 181 products; 35 collections organised by **type** (pocket squares, cravats, scarves, gift sets, four-in-one), **colour** (10: black, blue, brown, green, grey/silver, pink/peach, purple, red/maroon, white/ivory, yellow/gold), **pattern** (11: solid, polka-dot, paisley, floral, plaid/check, houndstooth, geometric, damask/brocade, calligraphy-art, heritage-ajrak, novelty), **occasion** (wedding, business-formal, casual-daytime, festive/Eid), and **curated edits** (luxe, new arrivals, gifts-for-him).
- **Pricing (verified range ≈£12–£82):** entry pocket squares ≈£17–£22; cravats/scarves mid-tier ≈£40–£55; scarf+square and Luxe sets ≈£52–£82. Free UK delivery over £50.

### Target audience (likely)
1. **Grooms & wedding parties (primary)** — 28–45, buying coordinated cravats/pocket squares for a wedding; high intent, gift-adjacent, price-tolerant.
2. **Style-conscious professionals** — 30–55, business-formal pocket squares/cravats for the office, races, and events.
3. **Gift buyers** — partners/family buying a "considered" gift for him (Father's Day, birthdays, Christmas, Eid).
4. **Heritage/cultural buyers** — South Asian diaspora and culturally-curious buyers drawn to Ajrak, calligraphy and poetry prints for Eid, mehndi/nikah, and meaningful gifting.
5. **Menswear enthusiasts / #menswear** — collectors who value hand-rolled edges, mulberry silk, and print stories.

### Current strengths
- Real product differentiation (heritage prints + British finish) and a coherent, broad catalogue.
- Strong site fundamentals: schema (Product/Collection/FAQ/HowTo/Article/Video), unique collection copy, optimised metadata, journal with 10 guides + video, wishlist/accounts.
- Attractive accessible-premium price position with free-shipping threshold that nudges AOV.
- Matching sets already exist — the exact SKU wedding parties and gifters want.

### Current weaknesses
- **Marketing/distribution near-zero:** no working social links, no email capture, no marketplaces, no visible paid/influencer/PR activity.
- **Brand split** across `.com` and `.co.uk`.
- **Story under-leveraged:** heritage not front-and-centre; "Our Story" is thin (~240 words) and a linking dead-end.
- **No social proof surfaced** (UGC, press, "as worn").
- **Conversion gaps:** no welcome offer, no urgency/scarcity, no trust microcopy at add-to-cart, recommendations rail is generic (see companion SEO/CRO audit).

### Conversion & visibility gaps (summary)
Traffic acquisition is the binding constraint, followed by capture (email) and trust (proof). The store is "conversion-ready enough" to justify pouring traffic in — but capture and proof must be fixed in the same sprint so that traffic isn't wasted.

## D. Competitor Strategy Breakdown

### Rampley & Co
- **Does well:** Elevates pocket squares to "wearable art." Each design has a narrative (fine-art reproductions, licensed museum imagery). Premium, consistent photography.
- **Drives visibility via:** PR around art/museum collaborations, a strong editorial Instagram, and brand partnerships that earn press.
- **Presents products:** Large, gallery-style imagery; provenance and print-story copy; gift-ready.
- **Offers/campaigns:** Limited-edition art drops, "back in stock" urgency, gifting.
- **Threaditionz should learn:** Tell the *print story* (Ajrak heritage, Ghalib poetry) the way Rampley tells the *art story* — and chase a cultural/heritage partnership (museum, V&A-style textile institution, designer collab) for PR.

### Cravat Club
- **Does well:** Owns the cravat + wedding niche with deep content and matching sets; ships in a luxury gift box (unboxing moment).
- **Drives visibility via:** Wedding-intent SEO/blog, social, and gifting positioning.
- **Presents products:** Pairings ("contrast & complement"), matching sets, lookbook.
- **Offers/campaigns:** Seasonal sales (up to 30% off), wedding-season pushes, gift box as standard.
- **Threaditionz should learn:** Build a wedding-season engine, merchandise matching sets aggressively, and add a premium gift-box/unboxing as a differentiator and UGC driver.

### OTAA
- **Does well:** Industrial-scale how-to content + email; groomsmen/wedding-party bulk ordering and wholesale.
- **Drives visibility via:** SEO content moat and email automation; weddings.
- **Threaditionz should learn:** A groomsmen bundle ("buy 4+, save"), a wedding-party concierge, and a content/email engine — but keep the premium silk story (don't copy the cotton/value tier).

### DQT
- **Does well:** Amazon marketplace presence + affordable wedding sets at volume.
- **Threaditionz should learn:** Use marketplaces for discovery — but list curated premium/heritage SKUs, not a price war.

### Drake's
- **Does well:** Aspirational editorial brand-building.
- **Threaditionz should learn:** Seasonal lookbooks and editorial photography to build brand equity over time.

### What NOT to copy
- DQT/Otaa's race-to-the-bottom pricing and synthetic fabrics — would destroy Threaditionz's premium/heritage equity.
- Over-discounting (erodes the accessible-premium position) — prefer bundles, gifting, and value-adds over blanket sales.

## E. Visibility Growth Strategy (by channel)

### Instagram (priority 1)
Fix footer links to `@threaditionz`. Post 4–5×/week: print-story carousels (Ajrak/Ghalib), fold/tie Reels, wedding flat-lays, "complete the look" trios, UGC. Use Shopping tags so every post is buyable. Run a branded hashtag (#ThreaditionzStyle). Stories: polls, "which print," behind-the-scenes finishing.

### TikTok (priority 1)
Educational + heritage storytelling: "How to fold a pocket square 4 ways," "How to tie a cravat," "The poem on this scarf" (Ghalib), "Get the groom ready." Lean into satisfying close-ups of silk/hand-rolled edges. Post 3–5×/week; repurpose to Reels.

### Pinterest (priority 1 — underrated for this category)
This is a gifting/wedding/style discovery engine with long content life. Build boards (see Section H), pin every product + journal image with keyword-rich descriptions, enable Rich Pins, and pin "how to fold/tie" infographics. Wedding and "groomsmen outfit" boards will compound.

### Facebook
Lower organic priority; use for retargeting + lookalikes via Meta Ads, a Shop catalogue, and wedding/gifting community groups. Maintain a basic presence and link it correctly from the footer.

### Meta Ads (priority 2)
Catalogue/Advantage+ shopping for prospecting; retarget viewers/add-to-cart. Creative angles: heritage story, "complete the wedding look," gifting, fold/tie how-to. Start small (see Section K).

### Google Shopping (priority 2)
Feed all 181 SKUs (Merchant Center) — high purchase-intent for "silk pocket square," "wedding cravat," "[colour] pocket square." Strong fit for a broad catalogue. Pair with Performance Max once the feed and pixel are healthy.

### TikTok Ads / Pinterest Ads (priority 3)
Spark Ads on best-performing organic TikToks; Pinterest ads on wedding/gifting pins seasonally.

### Email (priority 1)
Klaviyo (or similar): capture pop-up with welcome offer; flows for welcome, browse/cart abandonment, post-purchase (care guide + cross-sell), win-back; weekly campaign (new prints, occasion edits, journal).

### Influencers (priority 2)
Tiers: micro menswear/grooming creators (UK), wedding/groom creators, South Asian fashion & wedding creators (high-fit for heritage prints), and style/etiquette educators. Seed product; brief around fold/tie + heritage story + wedding.

### Affiliates (priority 3)
Recruit menswear bloggers, wedding planners, and style YouTubers via an affiliate program (10–15%); supply gift-guide-ready assets.

### Marketplaces (priority 2)
Etsy (wedding/personalised + heritage), Not On The High Street (wedding gifting), Amazon Handmade/UK (curated premium SKUs), eBay optional. Borrowed high-intent traffic while owned channels build.

### PR (priority 2)
Pitch the heritage angle (Ajrak/calligraphy/Ghalib poetry as wearable culture; British finish; immigrant-craft story) to menswear, wedding, and culture press; submit to gift guides (see Section H).

### Partnerships (priority 2)
Wedding suit-hire/tailors, menswear boutiques, wedding planners & venues, corporate gifting buyers, photographers/stylists, and cultural/heritage organisations.

### Offline / local activations (priority 3)
Pop-ups at wedding fayres and menswear/concept stores; consignment with UK boutiques and museum gift shops; craft and design markets; trunk shows with tailors.

## F. 90-Day Launch Plan

### Days 1–15 — Foundation & setup
- Fix footer social links to real profiles; claim/optimise Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook; set up Instagram/Facebook Shop + Pinterest Rich Pins.
- Install email platform; build capture pop-up + welcome offer; draft welcome, cart, post-purchase flows.
- Resolve `.com` vs `.co.uk` (canonical/market split); install Meta Pixel + Google tag + Merchant Center feed.
- Decide hero positioning (heritage-forward); shoot/curate a starter content bank (30 images, 10 videos) incl. fold/tie and print-story.
- Define the launch bundle (Wedding/Groomsmen set) and offer architecture.

### Days 16–30 — Content & audience building
- Begin organic posting cadence on IG/TikTok/Pinterest; publish heritage + fold/tie content; seed branded hashtag.
- Build Pinterest boards and pin full catalogue + journal.
- Start email list growth; send first newsletter (heritage story + new arrivals).
- List first 15–25 best-sellers on Etsy + Not On The High Street.
- Begin micro-influencer seeding (10–20 gifted creators), prioritising wedding + South Asian fashion.

### Days 31–45 — Soft launch campaigns
- Launch Wedding & Groomsmen campaign (landing page + bundle + email + organic).
- Turn on retargeting (Meta + Google) to existing/seeded traffic.
- Run first UGC push (incentivised hashtag); collect creator content.
- Pitch PR + submit to gift guides (Father's Day/seasonal as timing allows).

### Days 46–60 — Paid ads & influencer push
- Scale Meta Advantage+ shopping + Google Shopping/PMax with proven creative.
- Activate paid influencer collaborations (3–5 mid-tier) with trackable codes.
- Spark-Ads the top organic TikToks; seasonal Pinterest ads on wedding pins.
- A/B test offers (bundle vs free-gift vs threshold).

### Days 61–75 — Partnerships & marketplace expansion
- Onboard Amazon Handmade/UK with curated SKUs; expand Etsy/NOTHS ranges.
- Sign 2–3 partnerships (tailor/suit-hire, wedding planner, boutique consignment).
- Launch affiliate program; recruit first 10 affiliates.
- Run a wedding-fayre or boutique pop-up if feasible.

### Days 76–90 — Optimisation, retargeting & scaling
- Double down on winning channels/creatives; cut losers.
- Mature retargeting (dynamic catalogue, cart/lapsed segments) and email flows (win-back, replenishment/gift reminders).
- Plan seasonal calendar (Christmas gifting, Eid, wedding season, Ascot, Valentine's, Father's Day).
- Review KPIs; set Q2 growth roadmap.

**Launch KPIs to track:** email list size & capture rate, social followers & engagement, sessions by channel, add-to-cart & conversion rate, AOV (watch the £50 threshold), ROAS by channel, marketplace orders, UGC/creator pieces produced, PR placements.
